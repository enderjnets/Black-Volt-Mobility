## 1. Acceptance & Relationship of the Parties

1.1 By registering as a Driver and accepting these Terms, you agree to be legally bound by this Agreement.

1.2 **Independent contractor status.** You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Black Volt. You are engaged in your own independently established transportation business. Specifically, the parties agree that:

- (a) You are **free from Black Volt's control and direction** as to the *means and methods* of performing transportation services, both under this Agreement and in fact. Black Volt may specify the **result** (a passenger picked up and delivered safely, on time, to the agreed destination) but does not direct *how* you drive or operate your business;
- (b) You are **not required to work exclusively** for Black Volt and may provide services to others, including competitors;
- (c) Black Volt does **not** set a salary or hourly wage; you are paid an agreed **per-trip or contract rate** as set out in Schedule A, paid to your trade or business name where applicable;
- (d) Black Volt does **not** provide employee training beyond minimal orientation to its booking process and safety/compliance requirements;
- (e) Black Volt does **not** provide you tools, equipment, or employee benefits; you supply and maintain your own vehicle and equipment (or lease per a separate written agreement);
- (f) Black Volt does **not** dictate your working hours beyond the mutually agreed pickup times for trips you choose to accept; you may decline trips;
- (g) Your operations are **separate and distinct** from Black Volt's; and
- (h) This Agreement may **not** be terminated early at will; it may be terminated only for breach or failure to meet the agreed specifications (see §12).

## 2. Driver Eligibility, Representations & Warranties

You represent, warrant, and agree on an ongoing basis that you:

- Hold a valid Colorado (or reciprocal) driver's license of the proper class and are legally authorized to work in the United States;
- Own or lawfully control a vehicle that meets all legal and PUC requirements for the Service and keep it clean, safe, mechanically sound, and properly registered and inspected;
- Carry and maintain at all times the vehicle and commercial/for-hire insurance required by law and by Black Volt (see §5), and will name Black Volt as required;
- Have passed, and consent to ongoing, criminal-history background checks and fingerprint-based checks as required for the applicable PUC permit, and will promptly disclose any arrest or conviction;
- Are medically fit to drive and hold any required medical certification;
- Will comply with all hours-of-service limits and will not drive while fatigued, impaired, or otherwise unsafe;
- Have **no** disqualifying convictions (including, e.g., assault, harassment, kidnapping, menacing, stalking, domestic violence, sexual offenses, DUI within the disqualifying period, or violent or serious felonies) consistent with Colorado rideshare/carrier safety law;
- Will not allow anyone else to use your driver credentials or accept a trip on your behalf (no account sharing or "impostor" driving).

A material misrepresentation in this Section is grounds for immediate termination for breach.

## 3. Performance Specifications (Result, Not Method)

Black Volt may communicate the agreed **specifications** for each accepted trip — pickup time, location, destination, passenger count, vehicle class, and safety/compliance standards required by law. Subject to those specifications and the law, you control the route, the operation of your vehicle, and how you run your business. You will provide safe, professional, courteous service and comply with the non-discrimination and service-animal requirements in Black Volt's Passenger Terms.

## 4. Compliance With Law

You are solely responsible for complying with all federal, state, and local laws and all Colorado PUC, CDLE, and (if applicable) FMCSA rules that apply to your services, including licensing, insurance, vehicle inspection, hours-of-service, recordkeeping, and safety rules. You are responsible for your own business licenses, taxes, and filings, including all federal and state income and self-employment taxes; Black Volt will not withhold taxes and will issue a Form 1099 where required.

## 5. Insurance & Financial Responsibility

5.1 You will maintain, at your expense, the automobile liability and any for-hire/commercial coverage required by law and by Black Volt's PUC authority, with minimum limits of $500,000 combined single limit, and will provide certificates on request and name Black Volt as an additional insured where required.

5.2 You are responsible for any deductibles and for any loss not covered by insurance arising from your operation.

5.3 You will immediately notify Black Volt of any accident, injury, citation, insurance lapse, license suspension, or disqualifying event.

## 6. Indemnification by Driver

To the fullest extent permitted by law, you agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Black Volt and its members and agents from any claims, damages, fines, penalties, losses, or costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) your acts or omissions, negligence, or willful misconduct; (b) any accident, injury, or property damage occurring during your operation; (c) your breach of this Agreement or violation of law or PUC rules; (d) any claim by any person you hire, engage, or allow to assist you (you are solely responsible for your own workers, including their wages, taxes, and classification); and (e) your misrepresentation of eligibility. This Section survives termination.

## 7. Release & Acknowledgments Regarding Your Business

7.1 You acknowledge that Black Volt does **not** guarantee any volume of trips, level of earnings, or continued engagement, and that your income depends on trips you choose to accept and on market conditions.

7.2 To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release Black Volt from claims for lost business, lost profits, or lost opportunity arising from the volume of trips offered, trip cancellations, or termination consistent with this Agreement.

7.3 This release does **not**, and cannot, waive any non-waivable right you may have under Colorado wage-and-hour law, unemployment law, or workers'-compensation law **if** you are ever determined to be an employee. Those rights are protected by statute and cannot be contracted away.

## 8. Confidentiality, Client Non-Solicitation & Non-Circumvention

8.1 **Confidential information.** Black Volt's client list, contact details, booking records, pricing, business methods (including the booking protocol), and CRM data are confidential and the property of Black Volt. You will use them only to perform trips and will not copy, retain, disclose, or use them for any other purpose.

8.2 **Non-solicitation / non-circumvention.** During this engagement and for 12 months afterward, you will not solicit, divert, or knowingly accept private business from Black Volt clients whom you served or learned of through Black Volt, or otherwise circumvent Black Volt to transport its clients directly.

8.3 Black Volt's clients are Black Volt's clients, not yours, for purposes of this Agreement.

## 9. Intellectual Property & Data

The Black Volt name, logo, brand, and all booking and client data are owned by Black Volt. You receive no rights in them except a limited right to use them as needed to perform trips. You will protect passenger personal information and use it only to provide the Service.

## 10. Recording & Safety Cooperation

You will comply with Black Volt's in-vehicle recording and safety policies and applicable law, and will cooperate with safety investigations, complaint reviews, and lawful requests from regulators.

## 11. Assumption of Driving Risk

You understand that operating a motor vehicle for hire carries inherent risks, and you assume the ordinary risks of driving that are not caused by Black Volt's own fault. (This does not waive any non-waivable statutory right described in §7.3.)

## 12. Term & Termination

12.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

12.2 Consistent with your independent-contractor status, Black Volt may terminate **for breach or failure to meet agreed specifications**, including any safety violation, disqualifying background result, insurance lapse, account sharing, or material misrepresentation. Either party may also terminate on 30 days' written notice at the conclusion of outstanding accepted trips.

12.3 On termination you will promptly stop using Black Volt's brand and data, return or delete confidential information, and complete or safely hand off any accepted trips.

12.4 Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, and 13 survive termination.

## 13. Dispute Resolution; Governing Law; Arbitration; Class/Collective Waiver

13.1 **Governing law.** Colorado law governs, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.

13.2 **Informal resolution first.** Send written notice of any dispute to blackvoltmobility@gmail.com and allow 30 days to resolve it.

13.3 **Binding arbitration.** Any unresolved dispute will be resolved by final and binding individual arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its applicable rules, seated in Arapahoe County, Colorado.

13.4 **Class/collective-action waiver.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, disputes will be resolved **only on an individual basis**, and the parties waive any class, collective, or representative action.

13.5 **Attorneys' fees / venue.** For matters not subject to arbitration, exclusive venue is the state courts in Arapahoe County, Colorado; the prevailing party may recover reasonable fees and costs to the extent allowed by law.

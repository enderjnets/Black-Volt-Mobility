# Black Volt Mobility LLC — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0**

**Effective date:** the date of your acceptance or use of the service.
**Contact:** blackvoltmobility@gmail.com

Black Volt Mobility LLC ("Black Volt," "we," "us") operates a luxury limousine
transportation booking platform in Aurora and the Denver metropolitan area,
including Denver International Airport (DEN), under Colorado PUC #01756. This
Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the
choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account information.** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name,
  email address, and a Google account identifier. Drivers and staff are added by
  invitation.
- **Profile and contact details.** Phone number, preferred language, and any
  addresses you save.
- **Trip information.** Pickup and drop-off locations, dates and times, ride
  preferences, and trip history needed to book and fulfill your rides.
- **Payment information.** Payments are processed by our payment processor
  (Square). We do not store full card numbers; we retain limited transaction
  records (amount, date, status).
- **Communications.** Messages, calls, and SMS/email you exchange with us or your
  assigned driver, and your consent choices for SMS and email.
- **Device and usage data.** Basic technical data such as IP address, browser/user
  agent, and session cookies used to keep you signed in and operate the platform.
- **Legal-agreement records.** When you accept our Terms or a driver agreement, we
  record the document, its version, the date/time, your IP address, user agent,
  language, and (for drivers) the legal name you type as your electronic signature.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To create your account and provide, schedule, and fulfill transportation.
- To process payments and maintain transaction records.
- To communicate with you about bookings, including confirmations and reminders,
  and (with your consent) marketing or service messages.
- To add confirmed rides to the relevant calendar where that integration is
  enabled.
- To operate, secure, and improve the platform and prevent fraud or misuse.
- To comply with legal obligations, including Colorado PUC requirements, and to
  keep records of legal-agreement consent.

## 3. How We Share Information

- **With your assigned driver/carrier** as needed to fulfill your ride
  (e.g., name, pickup/drop-off, contact for that trip).
- **With service providers** that operate the platform on our behalf, such as our
  payment processor (Square), calendar, communications, and hosting providers,
  under appropriate confidentiality terms.
- **For legal reasons** when required by law, regulation, subpoena, or to protect
  the rights, safety, and property of Black Volt, our users, or the public.
- **In a business transfer** (merger, acquisition, or sale of assets).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Location Information

We use the pickup and drop-off locations you provide to quote and fulfill rides.
We do not continuously track your device location outside of what is needed to
book and complete a trip.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to
provide the service, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and
enforce our agreements. Legal-agreement consent records are retained for the
duration required to evidence your acceptance.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

- **Access and correction.** You can review and update your profile information in
  the app.
- **Communications.** You can opt out of non-essential SMS or email at any time;
  some service messages (e.g., booking confirmations) are necessary to use the
  service.
- **Deletion.** You may request deletion of your account and associated personal
  information, subject to records we must retain by law.
- To exercise any of these, contact blackvoltmobility@gmail.com.

## 7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and operate the platform. You can
control cookies through your browser, but disabling essential cookies may prevent
the service from working.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them. Unaccompanied minors are not transported.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect
your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we
cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
communicated in the app or by email, and the new version will be posted with an
updated version number.
